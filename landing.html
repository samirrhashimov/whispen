Whispen

"Read in Peace, Focus with Ease"

Whispen is a modern, minimalist PDF reading app that combines ambient sounds and customizable themes to enhance focus and relaxation.

Download APK Try on Web

Why Choose Whispen?

  • 📄 Mobile-friendly PDF viewer
  • Clean and distraction-free reading interface
  • 🎵 Ambient sound support (online & offline)
  • 🎨 Theme switching with customizable palettes
  • 📚 Library system and recent files history
  • 🗣️ Multilingual interface (English, Azerbaijan, Turkish, Russian, Spanish)

Roadmap

  • Multi Language support (EN,TR +3 more)
  • Beta Testing Phase
  • Note Taking Integration
  • User asset ownership system
  • Stable Beta
  • AI Assistant, AI Vocabulary, AI Summarizer

Who is Whispen for?

  • Students: Study long PDFs with peaceful background sound to stay focused.
  • Readers: Enjoy immersive reading with adjustable themes and no distractions.
  • Developers & Researchers: Read tech docs in a calm environment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the APK safe to install?

Yes, the APK is self-hosted and free of trackers or ads. Whispen open-source on GitHub. Attention! Download files only from official Whispen accounts and sites!

Where is my data stored?

Your notes and settings are stored locally in your browser using IndexedDB.

Do I need to log in?

No login is required.You can use the application demo without logging in. Some features may not be unlocked in the demo version.

Can I change the background sounds?

Yes, you can choose from multiple ambient sounds (rain, wind, cafe, and more).
