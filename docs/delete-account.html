Account Deletion Request

At Whispen (by Linza Apps), we value your privacy and provide a straightforward way to delete your account and associated data.

How to delete your account

Option 1: In-App Deletion (Recommended)

  1. Open the Whispen app or visit whispen.netlify.app.
  2. Log in to your account.
  3. Click the Settings (☰) icon.
  4. Scroll down to the Account Options section.
  5. Click on Delete Account.
  6. Enter your current password and type DELETE to confirm.

Option 2: Manual Request
If you are unable to access your account, you can request deletion by emailing us at linzaapps@gmail.com. Please use the email address associated with the account you wish to delete.

What data is deleted?

When you delete your account, the following data is permanently erased from our servers:

  • Your email address and authentication profile.
  • Your username.
  • Any saved notes and quotes.
  • Account settings and preferences.
Data Retention & Recovery:

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Once confirmed, all your data is immediately removed from our active database. We do not retain any personal data after your account is deleted, except where required by law (e.g., for financial records if applicable).

For more details, please review our Privacy Policy.
